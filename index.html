How it actually works.

01

The system watches for manipulation

Before anything else, price has to sweep real liquidity — a session high, a prior swing, a resting pool of stops. This is the market showing its hand, not a random candle.

02

Then it waits for confirmation

A single move isn't enough on its own. The system requires a second, independent condition to align in the same direction before anything is considered valid — a precise, structured check against real market behavior, not a single-point guess.

03

Entry, stop, and target are all structural

The stop is placed at the point that actually invalidates the idea, not an arbitrary distance. The target is drawn from real liquidity sitting ahead of price — a higher-timeframe order block, an unfilled gap, a session extreme — not a fixed number of points.

04

Every rule is tested against real historical data

The framework isn't adjusted after the fact based on a handful of recent trades. Every condition has been built and checked against extensive historical market data first, so what you're seeing live is a consistent, structured process — not something reactive or improvised.

05

Alerts are sent as limit orders, not chases

Rather than firing the moment price is at a level, the system proposes the level itself — giving you time to actually place the order before price arrives, instead of reacting to something that already happened.
